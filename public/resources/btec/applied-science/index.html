BTEC Applied Science Resources

BTEC Applied Science revision notes, worksheets, past papers and exam practice from the existing JD Science resource library.

BTEC Applied Science is a vocational route, so this page focuses on practical scientific knowledge rather than a school GCSE layout. It opens the same JD Science resource browser used for other levels, already pointed at BTEC Applied Science. Use it for specifications, worksheets, exam-style questions and any videos that have been published for the course. Pearson is the board listed in the current catalogue. Related science pages for T-Level Science and GCSE Chemistry are included because many learners move between vocational and GCSE content. Files are not copied onto this page. When you choose a category, you continue in the live library, including any later admin uploads. Students who want tutoring in Applied Science can use Find a Tutor or Joseph Danso’s science lecturer profile.
