Hello and welcome back to JD Science. Today we are exploring reproduction. Human egg and sperm cells each have 23 chromosomes. When they fuse, the zygote has 46 chromosomes, which are 23 pairs. Mitosis makes identical cells for growth and repair. Myosis makes gametes with half the chromosomes and each gamete is genetically different. Sexual reproduction needs two parents and produces variation. Asexual reproduction needs one parent and produces clones. Genes are sections of DNA on chromosomes and each gene codes for a protein. DNA is a double helix made of nucleotides and bases pair as C with G and A with T. For genetics, each parent passes on one allele of each gene. Dominant alleles show up even when only one copy is present. Recessive alleles only show when two copies are present. Cystic fibrosis is recessive. Polydactyly is dominant. Sex is determined by the X and Y chromosomes. Myosis reduces chromosome number and generates genetic variation through processes including recombination and independent assortment. Fertilization restores the diploid chromosome number. In inheritance questions, always define the allele symbols before completing a Punnett square. A dominant allele is expressed in a heterozygote. This does not mean it is necessarily more common in the population. As you revise this topic, try to explain the key process in your own words.
