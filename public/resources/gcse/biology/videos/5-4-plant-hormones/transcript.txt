Hello and welcome back to JD Science. Today we are exploring plant hormones. We respond to light and gravity by growth. Shoots grow towards light, which is positive phototropism and against gravity, which is negative geotropism. Roots grow away from light and towards gravity. In shoots, auxin is destroyed by light, so the shaded side grows faster and the shoot bends towards the light. Etheen helps fruit ripen. Giborelens helps seeds germinate. Auxins are used as weed killers and rooting powders. Giborelens can end seed dormancy, promote flowering, and increase fruit size. In a shoot exposed to light from one side, auxin is redistributed so that more accumulates on the shaded side. Auxin promotes cell elongation in shoots, causing that side to grow faster and the shoot to bend towards the light. It is misleading to say that light simply destroys all auxin on the illuminated side. In roots, higher auxin concentrations can inhibit elongation, helping explain different growth responses to gravity. As you revise this topic, try to explain the key process in your own words.
