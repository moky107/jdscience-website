A-Level Physics Resources

A-Level Physics revision notes, worksheets, past papers and exam practice from the existing JD Science resource library.

This A-Level Physics page is for mechanics, materials, electricity, waves, fields and nuclear topics. It sends you into the existing JD Science resource browser rather than creating a second copy of each paper or worksheet. Start with revision notes or worksheets, then use past questions and mark schemes to check method and significant figures. Examiner reports help you see the mistakes that cost marks. Specifications remain listed so you can confirm practicals and mathematical requirements. Filter by AQA, Edexcel or OCR, and keep Eduqas or WJEC in the live tool if that is your board. Related pages for A-Level Chemistry, A-Level Biology and GCSE Physics sit below so you can move between courses without returning to a generic menu. Tutoring requests go through Find a Tutor or Joseph Danso’s profile.
