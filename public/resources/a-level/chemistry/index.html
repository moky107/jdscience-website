A-Level Chemistry Resources

A-Level Chemistry revision notes, worksheets, past papers and exam practice from the existing JD Science resource library.

A-Level Chemistry needs more than a single mixed resources list. This page opens physical, inorganic and organic chemistry through the same JD Science library that powers the past papers tool. Use worksheets for structured practice, then move to past questions and mark schemes when you want exam timing and command-word accuracy. Specifications and examiner reports stay in their own sections so you can see how papers are set. AQA, Edexcel and OCR are the usual board routes; Eduqas and WJEC remain available in the live browser where those entries exist. Nothing here replaces an uploaded file or a Supabase record. It is a crawlable doorway into that existing set. Students who want tutoring after independent work can continue to Find a Tutor or read Joseph Danso’s science lecturer profile.
