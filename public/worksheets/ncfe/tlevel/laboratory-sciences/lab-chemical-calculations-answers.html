Answer sheet

Laboratory chemical calculations

Separate mark scheme — do not issue with the student worksheet
Time guide: — · Questions: 32 · Total marks: 104
Written in occupational specialism style (PO1–PO3 / Assignments 1–3). Refer to SOPs, COSHH, GLP and LIMS.

Indicative marking points. Award marks for equivalent scientific or mathematical wording. Do not issue this sheet with the student worksheet.

1.
State what is meant by n = m/M.
[1 mark]
  • moles = mass / molar mass
2.
State what is meant by c = n/V.
[1 mark]
  • concentration in mol dm⁻³
3.
State what is meant by a dilution.
[1 mark]
  • c1V1 = c2V2
4.
State what is meant by a titration calculation.
[1 mark]
  • moles of known, ratio, moles of unknown, then c or m
5.
State what is meant by percentage yield.
[1 mark]
  • actual / theoretical × 100
6.
State what is meant by atom economy.
[1 mark]
  • Mr desired / Mr all products × 100
7.
State what is meant by a standard solution.
[1 mark]
  • known accurate concentration
8.
State what is meant by uncertainty.
[1 mark]
  • combine pipette, flask and burette errors
9.
State what is meant by a calibration graph.
[1 mark]
  • absorbance vs concentration
10.
State what is meant by unit prefixes.
[1 mark]
  • m, µ, n, k, M

17.
Dilute 10.0 cm³ of 1.00 mol dm⁻³ to 250 cm³. New c?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 0.0400 mol dm⁻³
18.
25.0 cm³ of 0.100 mol dm⁻³ HCl vs NaOH. Moles HCl?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 0.00250 mol
19.
If that NaOH titre is 20.0 cm³, c(NaOH)?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 0.125 mol dm⁻³
20.
Mass of 0.0500 mol of H₂SO₄ (M=98)
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 4.90 g
21.
Convert 250 µg to grams
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 2.50 × 10⁻⁴ g
22.
A graph reads 0.35 absorbance = 7.0 mg dm⁻³. What is 0.70 if linear through 0?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 14 mg dm⁻³
