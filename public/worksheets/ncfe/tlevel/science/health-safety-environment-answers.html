Answer sheet

Health, safety and environmental regulations

Separate mark scheme — do not issue with the student worksheet
Time guide: — · Questions: 32 · Total marks: 104
Written in T-Level core exam style (Papers A and B). Use the command verbs: state, describe, explain, calculate, evaluate. Show working and refer to workplace practice.

Indicative marking points. Award marks for equivalent scientific or mathematical wording. Do not issue this sheet with the student worksheet.

1.
State what is meant by COSHH.
[1 mark]
  • Control of Substances Hazardous to Health
2.
State what is meant by a hazard.
[1 mark]
  • something with the potential to cause harm
3.
State what is meant by risk.
[1 mark]
  • likelihood × severity of harm
4.
State what is meant by the hierarchy of control.
[1 mark]
  • eliminate, substitute, engineer, administer, PPE
5.
State what is meant by PPE.
[1 mark]
  • personal protective equipment
6.
State what is meant by a safety data sheet.
[1 mark]
  • supplier information on a chemical
7.
State what is meant by RIDDOR.
[1 mark]
  • Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences
8.
State what is meant by waste streams.
[1 mark]
  • clinical, chemical, sharps, general, recycling
9.
State what is meant by an environmental permit / duty of care.
[1 mark]
  • rules on storing and transferring waste
10.
State what is meant by an emergency procedure.
[1 mark]
  • spill, fire, first aid, evacuation
