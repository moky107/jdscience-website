AQA GCSE Biology (8461)
GCSE Biology
Answer sheet

Homeostasis and response

Separate mark scheme — do not issue with the student worksheet
Time guide: — · Questions: 32 · Total marks: 104
Written in AQA style. Use AQA command words. Quality of written communication is assessed on the 6-mark question.

Indicative marking points. Award marks for equivalent scientific or mathematical wording. Do not issue this sheet with the student worksheet.

1.
State what is meant by homeostasis.
[1 mark]
  • maintaining a constant internal environment
2.
State what is meant by a receptor.
[1 mark]
  • a cell that detects a stimulus
3.
State what is meant by a reflex arc.
[1 mark]
  • a rapid automatic response that does not involve conscious thought
4.
State what is meant by a synapse.
[1 mark]
  • a gap between two neurones
5.
State what is meant by the eye.
[1 mark]
  • sense organ that detects light
6.
State what is meant by accommodation.
[1 mark]
  • changing the lens shape to focus near or distant objects
7.
State what is meant by insulin.
[1 mark]
  • hormone from the pancreas that lowers blood glucose
8.
State what is meant by glucagon.
[1 mark]
  • hormone that raises blood glucose
9.
State what is meant by Type 2 diabetes.
[1 mark]
  • the body cells no longer respond properly to insulin
10.
State what is meant by auxin.
[1 mark]
  • plant hormone that controls phototropism
11.
Amira is asked about homeostasis in a AQA Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • includes temperature, blood glucose, water
(b) [3 marks]
  • why homeostasis is needed
12.
Amira is asked about a receptor in a AQA Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • part of a reflex arc
(b) [3 marks]
  • why receptors are specific
13.
Amira is asked about a reflex arc in a AQA Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • receptor → sensory neurone → synapse in CNS → motor neurone → effector
(b) [3 marks]
  • why reflexes are fast
14.
Amira is asked about a synapse in a AQA Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • neurotransmitter diffuses across
(b) [3 marks]
  • why synapses only transmit in one direction
15.
Amira is asked about the eye in a AQA Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • retina contains receptors
(b) [3 marks]
  • why the pupil changes size
16.
Amira is asked about accommodation in a AQA Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • ciliary muscles and suspensory ligaments
(b) [3 marks]
  • why a convex lens is needed for long sight
17.
A reflex takes 0.018 s to travel 1.8 m. Calculate the speed of the impulse.
[3 marks]
  • 1.8/0.018
  • Final answer: 100 m/s
18.
Blood glucose rises from 4.0 to 6.2 mmol/dm³. Calculate the percentage increase.
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 55%
19.
A person produces 1.5 dm³ of urine a day. How much in a week?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 10.5 dm³
20.
Reaction time is 0.28 s. How far does a car travelling at 15 m/s travel in that time?
[3 marks]
  • s=vt
  • Final answer: 4.2 m
21.
A plant shoot bends 12° in 40 minutes. Calculate the mean rate.
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 0.3 °/min
22.
Body temperature falls from 37.0°C to 35.5°C. Calculate the drop as a percentage of 37.0°C.
[3 marks]
  • 1.5/37 × 100
  • Final answer: 4.05%
23.
Amira carries out the reaction-time practical (ruler drop). This is a typical AQA required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: practice / caffeine / distraction
  • Dependent: catch distance / calculated time
(b) [2 marks]
  • same ruler, same hand
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • anticipating the drop
24.
Amira carries out investigating phototropism with seedlings. This is a typical AQA required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: direction of light
  • Dependent: direction of growth
(b) [2 marks]
  • same species and age
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • pots not rotated equally in the control
25.
Amira carries out testing urine or a model for glucose. This is a typical AQA required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: sample
  • Dependent: Benedict's colour
(b) [2 marks]
  • same heating time
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • not using a water bath safely
26.
Amira carries out the human nervous system observation of pupil reflex (light). This is a typical AQA required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: light intensity
  • Dependent: pupil diameter
(b) [2 marks]
  • same eye, same starting light
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • measuring a moving pupil
27.
Compare the nervous system with the endocrine system.
[4 marks]
  • nervous: fast, electrical, short-lived
  • endocrine: slower, chemical hormones, longer lasting
28.
Compare Type 1 diabetes with Type 2 diabetes.
[4 marks]
  • Type 1: no insulin produced, insulin injections
  • Type 2: cells do not respond, diet/exercise/drugs
29.
Compare rods with cones.
[4 marks]
  • rods: low light, black and white
  • cones: colour, bright light
30.
Compare phototropism with gravitropism.
[4 marks]
  • phototropism is a response to light
  • gravitropism is a response to gravity
31.
Apply your knowledge of insulin to this situation: the pancreas does not produce enough insulin
[3 marks]
  • the pancreas does not produce enough insulin
32.
Apply your knowledge of glucagon to this situation: they have opposite effects
[3 marks]
  • they have opposite effects
