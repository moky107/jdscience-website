Unit 6 answers — Organic chemistry and fuels

Separate mark scheme — do not issue with the student worksheet
Time guide: — · Questions: 32 · Total marks: 104
Written in WJEC style. Show working and give units.

Indicative marking points. Award marks for equivalent scientific or mathematical wording. Do not issue this sheet with the student worksheet.

1.
State what is meant by a hydrocarbon.
[1 mark]
  • compound of H and C only
2.
State what is meant by an alkane.
[1 mark]
  • saturated hydrocarbon CₙH₂ₙ₊₂
3.
State what is meant by an alkene.
[1 mark]
  • unsaturated hydrocarbon CₙH₂ₙ
4.
State what is meant by fractional distillation.
[1 mark]
  • separating crude oil by boiling point
5.
State what is meant by cracking.
[1 mark]
  • breaking long alkanes into shorter alkanes and alkenes
6.
State what is meant by complete combustion.
[1 mark]
  • hydrocarbon + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O
7.
State what is meant by incomplete combustion.
[1 mark]
  • produces CO and/or C
8.
State what is meant by a polymer.
[1 mark]
  • long chain of monomers
9.
State what is meant by a functional group.
[1 mark]
  • the reactive part of a molecule
10.
State what is meant by an ester.
[1 mark]
  • from alcohol + carboxylic acid
11.
Owain is asked about a hydrocarbon in a WJEC Chemistry paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • alkanes and alkenes
(b) [3 marks]
  • crude oil is a mixture of hydrocarbons
12.
Owain is asked about an alkane in a WJEC Chemistry paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • single C–C bonds only
(b) [3 marks]
  • used as fuels
13.
Owain is asked about an alkene in a WJEC Chemistry paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • contains C=C
(b) [3 marks]
  • bromine water decolourises
14.
Owain is asked about fractional distillation in a WJEC Chemistry paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • fractionating column
(b) [3 marks]
  • larger molecules have higher BP
15.
Owain is asked about cracking in a WJEC Chemistry paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • high T and catalyst
(b) [3 marks]
  • meets demand for petrol and alkenes
16.
Owain is asked about complete combustion in a WJEC Chemistry paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • blue flame
(b) [3 marks]
  • plenty of oxygen
17.
Write the formula of the alkane with 9 carbons.
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: C9H20
18.
Balance C₃H₈ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O
19.
Mr of C₂H₄ (C=12, H=1)
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 28
20.
A 100 cm³ sample of alkene decolourises bromine water. What does this show?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: C=C present / unsaturated
21.
Percentage of C in CH₄
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 75%
22.
Moles of CO₂ from 2 mol C₂H₆ complete combustion (2C₂H₆ + 7O₂ → 4CO₂ + 6H₂O)
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 4 mol
