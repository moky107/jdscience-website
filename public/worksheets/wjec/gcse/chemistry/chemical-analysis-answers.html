Chemical analysis

Separate mark scheme — do not issue with the student worksheet
Time guide: — · Questions: 32 · Total marks: 104
Written in WJEC style. Show working and give units.

Indicative marking points. Award marks for equivalent scientific or mathematical wording. Do not issue this sheet with the student worksheet.

1.
State what is meant by a pure substance.
[1 mark]
  • single element or compound / sharp melting point
2.
State what is meant by a formulation.
[1 mark]
  • mixture designed as a useful product
3.
State what is meant by Rf value.
[1 mark]
  • distance spot ÷ distance solvent front
4.
State what is meant by chromatography.
[1 mark]
  • separating dissolved substances
5.

  • identifies some metal ions by colour

  • machines such as flame photometers

17.
Rf = 4.2 / 6.0
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 0.70
18.
A solvent front is 8.0 cm and Rf is 0.25. How far did the spot travel?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 2.0 cm
19.
How many ions are tested if five flame colours are recorded?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: five metal ions
20.
A solution gives a white ppt with acidified BaCl₂. Which ion?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: sulfate
21.
A solution gives a cream ppt with AgNO₃ that dissolves in concentrated NH₃. Which halide?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: bromide
22.

[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 95%
