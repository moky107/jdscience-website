GCSE Biology
Answer sheet

Respiration and photosynthesis

Separate mark scheme — do not issue with the student worksheet
Time guide: — · Questions: 32 · Total marks: 104
Written in WJEC style. Answer in full sentences unless a calculation is required.

Indicative marking points. Award marks for equivalent scientific or mathematical wording. Do not issue this sheet with the student worksheet.

1.
State what is meant by photosynthesis.
[1 mark]
  • endothermic reaction that uses light to make glucose from CO₂ and water
2.
State what is meant by a limiting factor.
[1 mark]
  • a factor in shortest supply that holds the rate down
3.
State what is meant by chlorophyll.
[1 mark]
  • green pigment that absorbs light
4.
State what is meant by glucose in plants.
[1 mark]
  • product used for respiration, stored as starch, used to make cellulose, proteins and oils
5.
State what is meant by aerobic respiration.
[1 mark]
  • exothermic reaction using oxygen to release energy from glucose
6.
State what is meant by anaerobic respiration in muscle.
[1 mark]
  • incomplete breakdown of glucose without oxygen, producing lactic acid
7.
State what is meant by anaerobic respiration in yeast.
[1 mark]
  • fermentation producing ethanol and CO₂
8.
State what is meant by the stomata.
[1 mark]
  • pores in the leaf that allow gas exchange
9.
State what is meant by xylem.
[1 mark]
  • tissue that transports water and minerals from roots to leaves
10.
State what is meant by phloem.
[1 mark]
  • tissue that transports dissolved sugars
11.
Owain is asked about photosynthesis in a WJEC Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • occurs in chloroplasts
(b) [3 marks]
  • why the rate levels off at high light intensity
12.
Owain is asked about a limiting factor in a WJEC Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • light, CO₂ or temperature for photosynthesis
(b) [3 marks]
  • why growers increase CO₂ in a greenhouse
13.
Owain is asked about chlorophyll in a WJEC Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • found in chloroplasts
(b) [3 marks]
  • why a plant with yellow leaves photosynthesises more slowly
14.
Owain is asked about glucose in plants in a WJEC Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • can be converted to sucrose for transport
(b) [3 marks]
  • why a starch test is used after a photosynthesis experiment
15.
Owain is asked about aerobic respiration in a WJEC Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • produces CO₂ and water
(b) [3 marks]
  • why more energy is released than in anaerobic respiration
16.
Owain is asked about anaerobic respiration in muscle in a WJEC Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • releases less energy
(b) [3 marks]
  • why an oxygen debt builds up
17.
A plant produces 12 cm³ of oxygen in 4 minutes. Calculate the rate.
[3 marks]
  • 12/4
  • Final answer: 3 cm³/min
18.
Light intensity is estimated as 1/d². If d = 40 cm, calculate 1/d².
[3 marks]
  • inverse square
  • Final answer: 6.25e-4
19.
Respiration releases 1200 J from 0.5 g of glucose. Calculate J per gram.
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 2400 J/g
20.
A leaf loses 0.8 g of water in 10 minutes. Calculate the transpiration rate in g/h.
[3 marks]
  • ×6
  • Final answer: 4.8 g/h
21.
Temperature rises from 10°C to 20°C and rate doubles. What would you expect from 20°C to 30°C if Q10 = 2, before denaturation?
[3 marks]
  • Q10 idea
  • Final answer: rate doubles again
22.
A potometer air bubble moves 40 mm in 2 minutes. Calculate the mean speed.
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 20.0 mm/min
23.
Owain carries out the photosynthesis required practical with pondweed. This is a typical WJEC required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: light intensity / colour / CO₂
  • Dependent: bubbles per minute / volume of O₂
(b) [2 marks]
  • temperature, same length of pondweed
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • bubbles not the same size
24.
Owain carries out testing a leaf for starch. This is a typical WJEC required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: whether the leaf was destarched / covered
  • Dependent: iodine colour
(b) [2 marks]
  • same boiling time in ethanol
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • not destarching the plant first
25.
Owain carries out the potometer transpiration practical. This is a typical WJEC required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: wind / temperature / humidity / light
  • Dependent: distance moved by the bubble
(b) [2 marks]
  • same leafy shoot
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • air leak in the apparatus
26.
Owain carries out investigating respiration with germinating seeds and limewater or a respirometer. This is a typical WJEC required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: alive vs boiled seeds
  • Dependent: CO₂ produced / volume change
(b) [2 marks]
  • same mass of seeds
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • temperature change affects gas volume
27.
Compare photosynthesis with aerobic respiration.
[4 marks]
  • photosynthesis stores energy / endothermic
  • respiration releases energy / exothermic
  • equations are reverse
28.
Compare xylem with phloem.
[4 marks]
  • xylem: water, one way, dead
  • phloem: sugars, both ways, living
29.
Compare aerobic respiration with anaerobic respiration in muscle.
[4 marks]
  • aerobic: more ATP, CO₂ + water
  • anaerobic: lactic acid, oxygen debt
30.
Compare a palisade cell with a guard cell.
[4 marks]
  • palisade: photosynthesis
  • guard cell: controls stomatal opening
31.
Apply your knowledge of anaerobic respiration in yeast to this situation: CO₂ bubbles
[3 marks]
  • CO₂ bubbles
32.
Apply your knowledge of the stomata to this situation: reduce water loss
[3 marks]
  • reduce water loss
