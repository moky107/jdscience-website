WJEC GCSE Mathematics (3300)
GCSE Maths
Answer sheet

Number

Separate mark scheme — do not issue with the student worksheet
Time guide: — · Questions: 32 · Total marks: 79
Written in WJEC Higher-style. Show working. Give units where needed.

Indicative marking points. Award marks for equivalent scientific or mathematical wording. Do not issue this sheet with the student worksheet.

1.
Write 528 as a product of prime factors.
[2 marks]
  • Use a factor tree / repeated division
  • Final answer: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3 × 11
2.
Find the HCF and LCM of 44 and 60.
[3 marks]
  • HCF from common primes; LCM from all primes to highest power
  • Final answer: HCF = 4, LCM = 660
3.
Work out (4.4 × 10⁵) + (5.2 × 10⁴). Give your answer in standard form.
[3 marks]
  • Convert to the same power of 10, then add
  • Final answer: 4.92 × 10^5
4.
Work out (4.4 × 10⁴) × (5.2 × 10⁻³). Give your answer in standard form.
[2 marks]
  • Multiply numbers and add indices
  • Final answer: 22.9 × 10^1
5.
Convert 0.3131... to a fraction in its simplest form.
[3 marks]
  • Let x = recurring decimal, 100x − x = integer
  • Final answer: 31/99
6.
6.800000000000001 cm has been rounded to 1 decimal place. Write the error interval.
[2 marks]
  • 6.750000000000001 ≤ actual value < 6.8500000000000005
7.
A rectangle is measured as 12.8 cm by 5.4 cm, each to 1 d.p. Work out the upper bound of the area.
[3 marks]
  • Upper length × upper width
  • Final answer: 70.033 cm²
8.
A jacket is reduced by 20% in a sale and now costs £56. Work out the original price.
[3 marks]
  • Reverse percentage: sale price ÷ 0.8
  • Final answer: £70.00
9.
Owain invests £320 at 6% compound interest for 3 years. Work out the value of the investment.
[3 marks]
  • 320 × (1 + 6/100)^3
  • Final answer: £381.13
10.
The same £320 is invested at 6% simple interest for 3 years. Work out the interest earned.
[2 marks]
  • I = PRT/100
  • Final answer: £57.60
11.
A car worth £8800 depreciates by 15% a year for 2 years. Find its value after 2 years.
[3 marks]
  • Multiply by 0.85 twice
  • Final answer: £6358.00
12.
Work out 2 3/4 + 1 5/6. Give your answer as a mixed number.
[3 marks]
  • Convert to improper fractions, LCD 12
  • Final answer: 4 7/12
13.
Work out 3 1/5 ÷ 2/3.
[3 marks]
  • 16/5 × 3/2 = 48/10 = 24/5
  • Final answer: 4 4/5
14.
Write down the reciprocal of 0.2
[1 mark]
  • 5
15.
Simplify √104.
[2 marks]
  • Write as a product with a square factor
  • Final answer: 2√26
16.
Rationalise the denominator of 6 / √24.
[3 marks]
  • Multiply numerator and denominator by the surd
  • Final answer: 6 / √24 = 6√24 / 24, then simplify if possible (2√6)
17.
Work out 3⁷ ÷ 3⁴.
[1 mark]
  • Subtract indices
  • Final answer: 3³ or 27
18.
Work out 5⁻².
[2 marks]
  • Negative index means reciprocal
  • Final answer: 1/25
19.
Work out 27^(2/3).
[2 marks]
  • Cube root of 27 is 3, then square
  • Final answer: 9
20.
Estimate (39.6 × 19.8) ÷ 7.9 by rounding each number to 1 significant figure.
[2 marks]
  • 40 × rounded value ÷ 8
  • Final answer: 100
21.
Write 7/8 as a decimal and as a percentage.
[2 marks]
  • 0.875
  • 87.5%
22.
Share £260 in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5.
[3 marks]
  • Total parts = 10
  • Final answer: £52.00 : £78.00 : £130.00
23.
Round 0.04682 to 2 significant figures.
[1 mark]
  • 2nd sf is 6, next digit 8 so round up
  • Final answer: 0.047
24.
Work out (2.5 × 10⁷) ÷ (5 × 10³) in standard form.
[2 marks]
  • Divide numbers, subtract indices
  • Final answer: 5 × 10³
25.
A shop buys a lamp for £28 and sells it for £36. Work out the percentage profit.
[2 marks]
  • Profit ÷ cost × 100
  • Final answer: 29%
26.
Simplify (√16 + √2)(√16 − √2).
[3 marks]
  • Difference of two squares
  • Final answer: 14
27.
Owain writes 4.7 × 10ⁿ = 0.00047.
(a) [1 mark]
  • n = −4
(b) [2 marks]
  • 4.7 × 10⁵
28.
A number x is given as 100 to the nearest 10. Find the lower bound of 1000 / x.
[3 marks]
  • To minimise 1000/x use the upper bound of x
  • Final answer: 9.524
29.
Explain the difference between a terminating decimal and a recurring decimal. Give an example of each.
[3 marks]
  • Terminating stops, e.g. 0.25
  • Recurring repeats, e.g. 1/3 = 0.333...
30.
Work out 1 1/2 × 2 2/3.
[3 marks]
  • 3/2 × 8/3 = 4
  • Final answer: 4
31.
Here is a number machine: input → × 4 → − 7 → output.
(a) [1 mark]
  • 13
(b) [2 marks]
  • (29 + 7) ÷ 4 = 9
32.
In a WJEC calculator paper, a student writes 3.6 × 10⁴ + 2.1 × 10³ = 5.7 × 10⁷. Explain the mistake and give the correct answer in standard form.
[3 marks]
  • Powers of 10 were added incorrectly / numbers not converted to the same power
  • Correct: 3.81 × 10⁴
