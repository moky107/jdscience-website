OCR Gateway Science Biology A (J247)
GCSE Biology
Answer sheet

B6 Global challenges

Separate mark scheme — do not issue with the student worksheet
Time guide: — · Questions: 32 · Total marks: 104
Written in OCR Gateway A style. Use the data sheet where a calculation needs a formula.

Indicative marking points. Award marks for equivalent scientific or mathematical wording. Do not issue this sheet with the student worksheet.

1.
State what is meant by a community.
[1 mark]
  • all the populations of different species in a habitat
2.
State what is meant by an ecosystem.
[1 mark]
  • the community and the non-living conditions
3.
State what is meant by a producer.
[1 mark]
  • an organism that makes its own food by photosynthesis
4.
State what is meant by a predator-prey cycle.
[1 mark]
  • populations rise and fall out of step
5.
State what is meant by biodiversity.
[1 mark]
  • the variety of species in an area
6.
State what is meant by the carbon cycle.
[1 mark]
  • carbon moves between air, living things and fossil stores
7.
State what is meant by the water cycle.
[1 mark]
  • evaporation, transpiration, condensation, precipitation
8.
State what is meant by eutrophication.
[1 mark]
  • excess nutrients cause algal bloom, then oxygen crash
9.
State what is meant by a quadrat.
[1 mark]
  • a square frame used to sample organisms
10.
State what is meant by a transect.
[1 mark]
  • a line along which samples are taken
11.
Grace is asked about a community in a OCR Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • they interact
(b) [3 marks]
  • why removing one species can affect others
12.
Grace is asked about an ecosystem in a OCR Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • includes abiotic and biotic factors
(b) [3 marks]
  • why temperature is an abiotic factor
13.
Grace is asked about a producer in a OCR Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • start of a food chain
(b) [3 marks]
  • why all food chains start with a producer
14.
Grace is asked about a predator-prey cycle in a OCR Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • more prey → more predators → fewer prey
(b) [3 marks]
  • why predator numbers peak after prey
15.
Grace is asked about biodiversity in a OCR Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • high biodiversity makes an ecosystem more stable
(b) [3 marks]
  • why deforestation reduces biodiversity
16.
Grace is asked about the carbon cycle in a OCR Biology paper.
(a) [2 marks]
  • photosynthesis, respiration, combustion, decomposition
(b) [3 marks]
  • why burning fossil fuels increases CO₂
17.
A 0.25 m² quadrat contains 8 dandelions. Estimate the number in a 100 m² field.
[3 marks]
  • 8 × (100/0.25)
  • Final answer: 3200
18.
Five quadrats have 3, 5, 2, 6 and 4 plants. Calculate the mean.
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 4
19.
A food chain has 10 000 kJ in grass and 1000 kJ in rabbits. Calculate the efficiency of transfer.
[3 marks]
  • 1000/10000 × 100
  • Final answer: 10%
20.
Only 1% of 2 × 10⁶ kJ of sunlight becomes biomass in plants. How much is stored?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 2 × 10⁴ kJ
21.
A population of 200 deer increases by 15%. What is the new population?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 230
22.
Grace samples 10 quadrats of 1 m² in a 500 m² wood and finds a mean of 3 fungi. Estimate the total.
[3 marks]
  • 3×500
  • Final answer: 1500
23.
Grace carries out a random quadrat investigation of plant abundance. This is a typical OCR required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: area / mowing / light
  • Dependent: number of plants / percentage cover
(b) [2 marks]
  • same quadrat size
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • not using random numbers
24.
Grace carries out a transect up a rocky shore or across a field. This is a typical OCR required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: distance along the transect
  • Dependent: species abundance
(b) [2 marks]
  • same interval
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • uneven spacing
25.
Grace carries out measuring abiotic factors (light, temperature, pH, moisture). This is a typical OCR required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: location
  • Dependent: instrument reading
(b) [2 marks]
  • same time of day
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • shading the light meter with your body
26.
Grace carries out investigating decay with milk or compost and lipase / protease. This is a typical OCR required-practical style question.
(a) [2 marks]
  • Independent: temperature
  • Dependent: pH change / mass loss
(b) [2 marks]
  • same volume
  • so the test is fair / only one variable is changed
(c) [2 marks]
  • uneven mixing
27.
Compare biotic factors with abiotic factors.
[4 marks]
  • biotic: living, e.g. predation
  • abiotic: non-living, e.g. light
28.
Compare a food chain with a food web.
[4 marks]
  • chain is one pathway
  • web shows many linked pathways
29.
Compare decay with combustion.
[4 marks]
  • both release CO₂
  • decay is biological / combustion is burning
30.
Compare intensive farming with conservation.
[4 marks]
  • intensive: high yield, lower biodiversity
  • conservation: protects habitats
31.
Apply your knowledge of the water cycle to this situation: less transpiration
[3 marks]
  • less transpiration
32.
Apply your knowledge of eutrophication to this situation: decomposers use up oxygen
[3 marks]
  • decomposers use up oxygen
