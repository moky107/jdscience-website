Respiration and photosynthesis

Indicative marking points. Award marks for equivalent scientific or mathematical wording. Do not issue this sheet with the student worksheet.

1.
State what is meant by ATP.
[1 mark]
  • adenine, ribose, three phosphates
2.
State what is meant by glycolysis.
[1 mark]
  • glucose to 2 pyruvate in cytoplasm
3.
State what is meant by the link reaction.
[1 mark]
  • pyruvate to acetyl CoA
4.
State what is meant by the Krebs cycle.
[1 mark]
  • oxidises acetate, produces reduced coenzymes and ATP
5.
State what is meant by oxidative phosphorylation.
[1 mark]
  • electrons down the ETC, chemiosmosis
6.
State what is meant by photosynthesis light-dependent reaction.
[1 mark]
  • thylakoid, photolysis, ATP and reduced NADP
7.

[1 mark]
  • stroma, RuBP + CO₂ → GP → TP
8.

[1 mark]
  • light, CO₂ or temperature

[1 mark]
  • energy lost as heat, waste, not eaten

[1 mark]
