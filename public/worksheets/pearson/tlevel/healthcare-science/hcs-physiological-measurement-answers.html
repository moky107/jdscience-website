Physiological measurement

Separate mark scheme — do not issue with the student worksheet
Time guide: — · Questions: 32 · Total marks: 104
Written in Healthcare Science style. Link science techniques to patient care.

Indicative marking points. Award marks for equivalent scientific or mathematical wording. Do not issue this sheet with the student worksheet.

[3 marks]

18.

[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 400
19.
ECG paper 25 mm/s. 5 large squares is how many seconds?
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 1.0 s
20.
HR from R-R of 20 small squares (1 mm = 0.04 s): 60 / 0.80
[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 75 bpm
21.

[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 96.3 (or 96%)
22.

[3 marks]
  • Final answer: 120 min
