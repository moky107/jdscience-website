Pearson T Level Science — Food Sciences (Food OS)
T-Level Food Sciences
Answer sheet

Microbiology in the food and drink industry

Separate mark scheme — do not issue with the student worksheet
Time guide: — · Questions: 32 · Total marks: 104
Written in occupational specialism style. Link answers to the food supply chain and safety systems.

Indicative marking points. Award marks for equivalent scientific or mathematical wording. Do not issue this sheet with the student worksheet.

1.
State what is meant by a foodborne pathogen.
[1 mark]
  • microbe that causes illness via food
2.
State what is meant by a spoilage organism.
[1 mark]
  • makes food unacceptable but not always unsafe
3.
State what is meant by the danger zone.
[1 mark]
  • 5–63 °C where many bacteria grow fast
4.
State what is meant by water activity.
[1 mark]
  • available water for microbes
5.
State what is meant by pH and food.
[1 mark]
  • low pH inhibits many pathogens
6.
State what is meant by cross-contamination.
[1 mark]
  • transfer of microbes from raw to ready-to-eat
7.
State what is meant by a colony count.
[1 mark]
  • cfu per gram or ml
8.
State what is meant by hygiene swabbing.
[1 mark]
  • checks cleaning
9.
State what is meant by shelf-life.
[1 mark]
  • time the food stays safe and of quality
10.
State what is meant by pasteurisation.
[1 mark]
  • heat process that reduces pathogens

19.

20.

22.
