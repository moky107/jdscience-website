[23.32–30.24] Let's begin. What is atom economy? Atom economy is a measure of how efficiently the atoms of
[30.24–35.94] the reactants are used to produce the desired product in a chemical reaction. In some chemical
[35.94–41.50] reactions, more than one product is formed. One product may be useful while the others
[41.50–47.52] may be unwanted byproducts. A reaction with a high atom economy uses a large proportion
[47.52–52.92] of the reactant atoms to make the desired product. A reaction with a low atom economy
[52.92–58.54] produces a greater proportion of unwanted byproducts. Atom economy is particularly
[58.54–64.22] important in industrial chemistry because it helps manufacturers reduce waste and use
[64.22–70.06] raw materials more efficiently. How do we calculate atom economy? We use the following
[70.06–75.66] formula. Atom economy equals the relative formula mass of the desired product taking
[75.66–80.80] account of its coefficient in the balanced equation divided by the total relative formula
[80.80–87.18] mass of all products also taking account of their coefficients multiplied by 100. Before
[87.18–91.64] carrying out the calculation, always make sure the chemical equation is balanced.
[92.26–98.36] Let's look at an example. Worked example. Calcium carbonate decomposition. When calcium
[98.36–104.52] carbonate is heated, it causes to form calcium oxide and carbon dioxide. The balanced
[104.52–112.78] chemical equation is CaCO3 yields CaO plus CO2. Suppose calcium oxide is the desired
[112.78–119.18] product. Calculate the atom economy of this reaction. The relative atomic masses are calcium
[119.18–127.34] 40, carbon 12 and oxygen 16. First, calculate the relative formula mass of calcium oxide.
[127.34–135.74] Calcium oxide contains 1 calcium atom and 1 oxygen atom. 40 plus 16 gives 56. Next,
[136.12–143.22] calculate the relative formula mass of carbon dioxide. 12 plus 2 multiplied by 16 gives 44.
[143.76–150.34] Now calculate the total relative formula mass of the products. 56 plus 44 gives 100.
[150.94–155.72] Next, divide the relative formula mass of the desired product by the total relative
[155.72–163.74] formula mass of the products. 56 divided by 100 multiplied by 100 gives 56%. Therefore,
[164.06–171.14] the atom economy of this reaction is 56%. This means that 56% of the mass of the reactants
[171.14–178.38] is incorporated into the desired product, calcium oxide. The remaining 44% is incorporated
[178.38–183.30] into carbon dioxide. Practice question. Now it's your turn.
