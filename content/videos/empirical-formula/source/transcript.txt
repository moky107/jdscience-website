[30.94-36.84] An empirical formula shows the simplest whole number ratio of atoms of each element in a compound.
[37.46-42.88] For example, glucose has the molecular formula C6H12O6.
[42.18-42.90] C6H12O6.
[43.32-49.60] This means that one molecule contains six carbon atoms, twelve hydrogen atoms, and six atoms.

[57.08-61.50] Therefore the empirical formula of glucose is CH2O.
[62.04-70.36] Notice that an empirical formula shows the simplest ratio of atoms, rather than necessarily showing the actual number of atoms in a molecule.
