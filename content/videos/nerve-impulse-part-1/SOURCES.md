# Sources — Transmission of a nerve impulse / chemical synapse

## Scientific

- Pearson BTEC Level 3 National Applied Science, Unit 1 Principles and Applications of Science I, nervous coordination content.
- AQA A-level Biology 3.6.2.1 and 3.6.2.2 (nerve impulses; synaptic transmission), used for Level 3 depth and British exam wording.
- Standard membrane-physiology values taught at this level: resting potential about −70 mV; threshold about −55 mV; peak about +30 to +40 mV; 3 Na⁺ out / 2 K⁺ in per ATP; saltatory conduction at nodes of Ranvier.
- Acetylcholine example: voltage-gated Ca²⁺ entry, exocytosis, nicotinic ligand-gated cation channels, acetylcholinesterase hydrolysis to acetate and choline, choline reuptake and resynthesis with acetyl-CoA.
- JD Science reconstructed Boardworks A2 nervous-system diagrams on `cursor/boardworks-interactive-biology-e7a9` were used as a teaching-sequence reference. No Boardworks artwork was copied.
