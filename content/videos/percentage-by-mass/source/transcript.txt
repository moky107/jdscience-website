[26.14–27.92] What is percentage by mass?
[28.64–33.94] Percentage by mass tells us what proportion of the total mass of a compound comes from a
[33.94–39.90] particular element. For example, water contains hydrogen and oxygen. Although
[39.90–45.44] water contains two hydrogen atoms and only one oxygen atom, oxygen contributes
[45.44–54.56] most of the mass becauselandish by mass allows us to calculate exactly how much
[54.56–57.58] of the total mass is contributed by each element.
[58.60–63.50] How do we calculate percentage by mass? To calculate the percentage by mass of an
[63.50–68.32] element in a compound, we use the following formula. Percentage by mass
[68.32–73.64] equals the total relative atomic mass contribution of the element divided by
[73.64–78.96] the relative formula mass of the compound multiplied by 100. In simpler
[78.96–84.10] terms, we first calculate the relative formula mass of the whole compound. Next,
[84.10–88.82] we calculate the mass contribution of the element we are interested in. We then
[88.82–94.06] divide that contribution by the total relative formula mass and multiply by 100.
[94.74–101.02] Let's apply this method to an example. Worked example 1. Water. Calculate the
[101.02–106.86] percentage by mass of oxygen in water. The chemical formula of water is H2O.
[107.40–113.90] The relative atomic masses are hydrogen 1 and oxygen 16. First, calculate the
[113.90–120.92] relative formula mass of water. 2 multiplied by 1 plus 16 gives 18. Therefore
[120.92–126.90] the relative formula mass of water is 18. Next, identify the mass contribution
[126.90–133.14] of oxygen. There is one oxygen atom with a relative atomic mass of 16. Therefore
[133.14–140.36] the contribution of oxygen is 16. Now divide 16 by 18 and multiply by 100.
[140.82–146.20] This gives approximately 88.9 percent. Therefore oxygen makes up
[146.20–152.70] approximately 88.9 percent of the mass of water. Worked example 2. Carbon

[178.00–184.20] the mass contribution of carbon. There is one carbon atom so its contribution is
[184.20–192.64] 12. Divide 12 by 44 and multiply by 100. This gives approximately 27.3 percent.
